**Content Pages**

General Structure:
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/{PRIMARY}/{CONTENT_SLUG_1}/{CONTENT_ID_1}
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/{PRIMARY}/{CONTENT_SLUG_1}/{CONTENT_ID_1}/{CONTENT_SLUG_2}/{CONTENT_ID_2}
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/{PRIMARY}/{CONTENT_SLUG_1}/{CONTENT_ID_1}/{CONTENT_SLUG_2}/{CONTENT_ID_2}/{CONTENT_SLUG_3}/{CONTENT_ID_3}

Examples:
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/courses/mastering-blast-beats/15732
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/courses/mastering-blast-beats/15732/introduction-lesson/67394

- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/method/getting-started-on-the-drums/97341
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/method/getting-started-on-the-drums/97341/gear/73673
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/method/getting-started-on-the-drums/97341/gear/73673/tuning-your-snare-drum/24832

---

**Forum Pages**

General Structure:
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/forums
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/forums/{CATEGORY_SLUG}/{CATEGORY_ID}
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/forums/{CATEGORY_SLUG}/{CATEGORY_ID}/{THREAD_SLUG}/{THREAD_ID}

Examples:
- https://musora.com/{BRAND}/forums/general-drum-discussion/4/whats-the-one-tip-for-self-confidence/13097

Previous:
- https://www.drumeo.com/members/forums/threads/general-drum-discussion/4
('threads' needs to be removed from this layer of urls)
